I built the tracker to find one heartbeat. If I don't make it back, this is what it looks like:

  one channel, never changing: C-14
  5 pulses in every burst
  every 90 seconds exactly, in true time
  six beacons, the last confirmed at 21:07:15 UTC on 31 Oct

The observatory clocks do not agree, so raw times lie. Their offsets are on file.
Each beacon carried its own payload: hex that reads as text, and that text is Base64 of one word.
Every word is a flag. The first beacon in true time is the first piece of the way home, the sixth is the last.
Other chains look close, and their payloads decode too. They are not him. - Ned
